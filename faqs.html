Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our printing services

HomeFAQs
We offer a wide range of printing services including apparel sublimation (shirts, jerseys, hoodies, jackets), large format printing (tarpaulins, panaflex, signage, stand banners), commercial printing (flyers, brochures, calling cards, posters), and custom sticker production (vinyl stickers, die-cut stickers).
Production times vary by product. Apparel typically takes 3-7 business days, large format printing 1-3 business days, and commercial printing 1-3 business days. Custom or complex orders may require additional time. We always provide an estimated completion date when you place your order.
Yes! We have a professional design team that can create or refine your artwork. Whether you need a logo designed, artwork formatted for printing, or a complete layout created, we're here to help. Design fees are quoted based on the scope of work.
We accept AI, PSD, PDF, EPS, CDR, PNG, and JPEG formats. For best results, vector files (AI, EPS, PDF) are preferred. If you're submitting raster images, please ensure they are at least 300 DPI at actual print size.
Yes, we offer competitive pricing for bulk orders. The discount varies depending on the product type, quantity, and complexity. Contact us with your requirements and we'll provide a customized quotation.
Minimum order quantities vary by product. For apparel, our minimum is typically 10 pieces per design. For printing and stickers, we can accommodate smaller quantities. Contact us for specific product minimums.
Yes, we offer delivery within Metro Manila and nearby provinces. Delivery fees depend on the order size and location. We also offer installation services for large-format prints and signage. You may also pick up your order from our shop.
You can request a quote through our Inquiry page, call us directly, or send us an email. Please provide as much detail as possible including product type, quantity, size, and any specific requirements so we can give you an accurate quotation.
